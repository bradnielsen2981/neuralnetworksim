Neural Network Simulator

A neural network uses gradient descent to curve-fit data by adjusting its weights and biases in order to minimize the loss function. This process is called Back propagation. Click to place points on the 3D graph below. Adjust the Neural Network. When you are ready, click Train to observe how the Neural Network learns from your data.
